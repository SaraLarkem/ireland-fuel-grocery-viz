When petrol and diesel get more expensive, do groceries follow? And how long does it take?

Petrol Diesel Food
The short answer

Food price rises tended to follow fuel price rises by about 9–12 months, mainly during the 2021–23 price surge. And when fuel prices fell again, food prices stayed high.

01 · The big picture

Fuel prices spiked twice. Food rose slowly, and stayed high.

Fuel jumped in 2022 and again in 2026, then fell back. Food climbed from 2022 and has not come down.

02 · Major events

Fuel prices swing hard. Food prices barely move.

During the Ukraine invasion, petrol moved 7.6% and diesel 6.8% in a typical month. Food moved just 1.1%.

05 · Which groceries

Fresh food rose more than twice as much as long-lasting food

Perishable items rose 34.6% since 2016; shelf-stable items 14.6%. Both started climbing in 2022, after the fuel spike.

What it means for you

After a big fuel shock, expect food prices to feel it for months

Households

Plan the food budget for the months after a fuel spike, not only at the pump.

Businesses

Shops, hauliers and farmers can plan prices and stock ahead.

Policymakers

Time support, like the 2026 fuel tax cuts, before food costs build up.

Journalists

Explain rising grocery bills with evidence, without blaming fuel alone.

Still to watch: the 2026 fuel spike

Food prices haven't risen yet (data to September 2026). It is too early to tell whether they will follow this time.

Keep in mind
  • Linked, not proven cause. Wages, energy and packaging costs rose too.
  • The delay comes mostly from 2021–23. It may not repeat.
  • National averages. Your shop or region may differ.
